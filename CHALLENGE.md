# BREAK SIX TRAP.

**Find an escaping orbit. Find a seventh trap. Find a flaw in the computation. Prove the six traps are complete. Or discover something none of us have seen yet.**

## What counts

### Find a seventh trap
A valid new periodic cycle not equivalent by rotation or repetition to one of the six registered cycles. Supply the complete cycle and verify every step using the actual least prime divisors required by the rule.

### Find an escape
A rigorous proof of an unbounded orbit or unbounded family. A large peak, a long transient, a timeout, a memory limit, or an unfinished factorization is not a proof of escape.

### Prove the six
A valid proof that every integer `n >= 2` eventually reaches one of the six registered cycles. A finite cutoff, average behavior, or a density bound with an exceptional set does not finish the universal statement.

### Find an error
A mathematical, computational, or reproducibility flaw that affects a published claim. Provide enough detail to reproduce the failure.

### Find prior art
An exact or materially equivalent predecessor with credible historical priority. The current record says only that no exact predecessor was identified in the searches performed; historical originality is unverified.

### Extend it
A new theorem, bound, proof method, structural result, independent reproduction, or useful exact computation is welcome even if it neither proves nor disproves the conjecture.

## Submission standard

Use [SUBMIT_A_RESULT.md](SUBMIT_A_RESULT.md). Claims of proof, counterexample, or computational error should include exact statements, dependencies, and reproducible evidence.

If someone really breaks Six-Trap, **verify them and credit them.**
