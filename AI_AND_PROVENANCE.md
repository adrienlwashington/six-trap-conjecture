# Creator, provenance, and origin

## Creator

**Adrien Washington**  
Founder, President & CEO of Seltlox

Six-Trap is Adrien Washington's mathematical project. Seltlox is his company and affiliation; Seltlox is not presented as the author or mathematical validator of the conjecture.

## AI-assistance disclosure

Six-Trap was created and directed by Adrien Washington. AI tools assisted with mathematical exploration, computation, code development, derivations, and adversarial testing. The project is released openly so its claims can be independently examined, reproduced, challenged, or disproved.

## Public origin story

The project grew from sustained attempts to attack famous open problems, especially Collatz. Adrien eventually reversed the question: instead of indefinitely trying to solve somebody else's mathematical monster, could he create and aggressively attack one of his own? Six-Trap emerged from that reversal.

Collatz is origin and intuition, **not** a claim of mathematical equivalence, comparable difficulty, or a reduction between the problems.

## Authorship boundary

The project does not claim that Adrien performed every derivation unaided, that AI independently invented the project, or that AI proved something humans could not. The provenance is intentionally disclosed so the mathematics can be judged from the public rule, arguments, code, data, and reproducibility record.
