# Contributing and challenge-response policy

The goal is mathematical scrutiny, not agreement.

Useful contributions include independent reproductions, verified counterexamples, proofs, prior-art sources, bug reports, stronger bounds, structural results, and clearer reproduction instructions.

## Response policy

- **Serious mathematical criticism:** engage and reproduce.
- **Valid counterexample:** verify quickly, acknowledge publicly, and credit the finder.
- **Bug:** verify, correct visibly, and preserve version history.
- **Prior art:** investigate immediately and update originality language/credit if genuine.
- **Claimed solution:** request the proof, counterexample, or reproducible artifact before amplifying.
- **Extensions:** credit and link genuine work.

Personal insults and credential theater are not part of the technical workflow. The mathematics is public and independently checkable.
