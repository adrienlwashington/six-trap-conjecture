# Originality and limitations

## Originality status

The largest-proper-divisor function `D` is established mathematics. The recorded searches compared the Six-Trap recurrence with nearby divisor and integer-dynamics material. No exact predecessor for

```text
T(n) = n + D(n) - D(n+1)
```

was identified in those searches.

That result is **not** a certificate of global historical originality. Database access and search scope were incomplete, and private or differently described prior work could exist. The correct release wording is:

> **No exact predecessor identified in the searches performed; originality unverified.**

Credible prior art should be investigated and credited.

## Claims this release does not make

This package does **not** establish that:

- Six-Trap is proved, unbreakable, unsolvable, or impossible to break;
- Six-Trap is harder than Collatz or mathematically equivalent to Collatz;
- the recurrence is definitely historically original;
- no human can solve it or professional mathematicians have failed to solve it;
- AI invented or proved Six-Trap;
- Seltlox invented or validated the mathematics;
- the billion-seed census proves universal convergence;
- computational survival is proof;
- the 50-move density bound is a capture percentage or confidence probability;
- the work has external mathematical review or end-to-end proof-assistant verification;
- the recurrence supplies cryptographic security, a new physical law, a measured biological mechanism, or a demonstrated commercial advantage.

## Current mathematical limits

The retained work has not excluded all longer hidden cycles, all unbounded orbits, or all exceptional starts. It has not established density-one descent, a global smaller-connected-value theorem, a successful universal ranking function, or a complete all-primes/all-lengths cycle classification.

A failed internal method is not a theorem that the method class is impossible unless the preserved result explicitly proves such an obstruction.
