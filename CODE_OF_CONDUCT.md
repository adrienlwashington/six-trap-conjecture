# Code of Conduct

Six-Trap is an open mathematical challenge. The goal is rigorous scrutiny, not agreement.

## Expected behavior

- Attack the mathematics, computation, evidence, and reasoning — not the person.
- Make technical criticism as specific and reproducible as possible.
- Distinguish conjecture, proof, finite computation, heuristic evidence, and speculation.
- Credit genuine counterexamples, corrections, prior art, proofs, extensions, and independent reproductions.
- Be willing to revise a claim when the evidence requires it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, threats, or discriminatory abuse.
- Credential-based dismissal in place of mathematical argument.
- Knowingly fabricated computations, citations, or results.
- Spam or repeated disruption unrelated to the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository Issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Technical disagreement is welcome and can be blunt. The standard is simple: keep the dispute about the work and support claims with mathematics or reproducible evidence.

For contribution mechanics, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
